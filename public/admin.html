Archive or delete?

  • Archive hides a finished job from the list but keeps every detail. Tick Show archived to see them again, or press Restore from archive to put one back. Nothing is lost — use this for anything you are done with.
  • Delete removes an enquiry for good, and always asks you to confirm first. A recovery copy is kept in data/deleted-bookings.json in case you delete the wrong one.
  • Archive finished archives every delivered and cancelled job in one go.

Change the admin key by starting the server with ADMIN_KEY=yourkey node server.js. Enquiries are saved in data/bookings.json — back that file up, and keep this page unlisted.
