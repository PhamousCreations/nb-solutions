Questions

Good to know

How fast is the turnaround?

Standard is 48 hours from pickup to delivery. Express same-day/next-day service is available for +50%. Dry cleaning of suits and gowns may take 72 hours.

Is pickup and delivery really free?

Yes, on orders above GH₵ 100. Below that we add a flat GH₵ 20 for the round trip. Cleaning jobs (home, office, deep clean) always include pickup and delivery in the quote.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), card on delivery, or cash to the rider. Businesses can request monthly invoicing.

What if an item is damaged or lost?

We log every item at pickup. If something is damaged or missing, tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we'll repair, replace or compensate up to GH₵ 500 per order.

Do you clean for offices and Airbnbs?

Absolutely. We do recurring office cleaning, scheduled Airbnb turnarounds (linen change, towels, restocking) and can invoice monthly. Ask us for a walk-through quote.

Which areas do you cover?

See the list near the bottom of this page. If you're just outside our zones, WhatsApp us — we often add stops for larger orders.
