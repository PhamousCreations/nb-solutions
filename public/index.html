Questions

Good to know

Do you work with businesses, or only homes?

Both. We take on commercial and industrial contracts — offices, hotels, guesthouses, shops and industrial premises — as well as residential cleaning and household laundry. Contract clients can be invoiced monthly.

How much does cleaning cost?

Cleaning is quoted per site rather than at a flat rate, because an office, a guesthouse and a warehouse are very different jobs. We offer a free assessment, then send a written quote based on the size, scope and frequency of the work.

How does laundry pricing work?

Laundry is charged by weight or per item, using the rate card above. We weigh and count everything at pickup and confirm the total with you before we start. Hotel linen, uniforms and bulk laundry can be arranged on agreed contract rates.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently serve clients around Accra and Greater Accra, and we're expanding. Tell us where you are and we'll confirm whether we can reach you — for larger or recurring jobs we will often travel further.

Do you bring your own equipment and supplies?

Yes. Our team arrives with its own cleaning equipment and materials. If you'd prefer we use your products — for example on specialist surfaces or where staff have allergies — just tell us and we'll follow your instructions.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), cash, or bank transfer. Business and contract clients can be set up on monthly invoicing.

What if something is damaged?

We count and log laundry items when we collect them. If anything is damaged or missing, tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we'll resolve it with you straight away.
