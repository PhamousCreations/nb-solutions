Company profile

A Ghanaian company built around doing the job properly

Registered in Ghana

N&B Solutions is a registered company in Ghana, operating as an industrial and commercial business focused on cleaning and laundry services. We work with clients who don't have the time, equipment or resources to handle their own cleaning and laundry — offices, hotels, guesthouses, businesses and households alike.

We start by serving clients within our operating area, and will expand to other locations as our customer base and revenue grow. Our aim is straightforward: to do the job properly, on time, so our clients keep us for years rather than weeks.

Our objectives

  • Build a service company whose number one goal is exceeding our clients' expectations
  • Run a sustainable commercial and industrial business that lives off its own cash flow
  • Build long-term relationships with our clients
  • Secure regular cleaning contracts with offices, hotels and other businesses
  • Create more employment opportunities as the business grows
  • Expand our operations and service coverage over time

Questions

Good to know

Do you work with businesses, or only homes?

Both. We take on commercial and industrial contracts — offices, hotels, guesthouses, schools and industrial premises — as well as residential cleaning and household laundry. Contract clients can be invoiced monthly.

How do we get started?

Send us a message through the contact form or WhatsApp, telling us what you need and where you are. For cleaning jobs we'll arrange a free site assessment, then agree the scope and schedule with you. Laundry can usually be arranged straight away.

Do you charge for the assessment?

No. We visit, look at the space or the workload, and discuss it with you at no cost and with no obligation. You'll know exactly what's involved before you commit to anything.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Apollo, Takoradi and cover the Western Region — Effia-Nkwanta, Kwesimintsim, Anaji, Airport Ridge, Apremdo, Beach Road, Sekondi, Ketan and Essikado among them. Tell us where you are and we'll confirm — for larger or recurring jobs we'll travel further.

Can you handle a large site or a standing contract?

Yes — that's the core of the business. We take on industrial and commercial premises with daily, weekly or fortnightly schedules, and we're set up for monthly invoicing. Tell us about the site and we'll come and assess it.

Do you bring your own equipment and supplies?

Yes. Our team arrives with its own cleaning equipment and materials. If you'd prefer we use your products — for example on specialist surfaces or where staff have allergies — just tell us and we'll follow your instructions.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), cash, or bank transfer. Business and contract clients can be set up on monthly invoicing.

What if something is damaged?

We count and log laundry items when we collect them. If anything is damaged or missing, tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we'll resolve it with you straight away.
